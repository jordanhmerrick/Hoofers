Correcting the record

The hole shape has nothing to do with where you physically walk. You walk wherever you want — round the block, up and down a hallway, on a treadmill. The phone counts steps, not places.

Hole 9 does not loop back to your front door. It loops in the game world, on a screen, while you walk anywhere at all. That was my mistake, twice over, and it's out of the design.

Why the holes bend. In other golf games, cutting the corner buys you strokes. In Hoofers it buys you footsteps — carry the pond on 7 and you walk 140 feet instead of 223. A straight hole can't offer that deal, which is the real reason none of these are straight.

Still a blockout. Untextured geometry, no art pass. The shapes are the point here, not the finish.
